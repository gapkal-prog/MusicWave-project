About

Arctic Monkeys are an English rock band formed in Sheffield in 2002. The group consists of Alex Turner (vocals, guitar), Jamie Cook (guitar), Matt Helders (drums), and Nick O'Malley (bass). Their debut album, Whatever People Say I Am, That's What I'm Not, became the fastest-selling debut album in UK chart history. Known for their razor-sharp lyrics and genre-defying evolution, the band has established themselves as one of the most important rock acts of the 21st century.
