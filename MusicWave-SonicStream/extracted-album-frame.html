AM is the fifth studio album by the English rock band Arctic Monkeys. The album features a heavier, more blues-influenced sound than its predecessors, drawing inspiration from hip-hop and R&B beats. It became the band's highest-charting album in the United States, debuting at number six on the Billboard 200.
