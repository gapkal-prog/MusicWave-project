EDITION #042

Recorded between damp neon alleyways in Euljiro and analog tape studios in Hannam-dong, NEO SEOUL SOUNDSCAPES explores the nocturnal pulse of Seoul. Combining gritty Rhodes electric piano, modular sub-bass, and rain-soaked street ambiance captured on binaural field microphones.
