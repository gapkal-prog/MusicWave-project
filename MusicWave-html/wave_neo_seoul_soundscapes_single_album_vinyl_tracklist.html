Curator's Liner Notes

Recorded between damp neon alleyways in Euljiro and analog tape studios in Hannam-dong, NEO SEOUL SOUNDSCAPES explores the melancholic nocturnal pulse of Seoul through vintage analog synthesizers, tape compression, and late-night R&B cadence.

“Every street corner in this metropolis hums in a distinct pitch. This album is our sonic cartography of those forgotten 3 AM frequencies.” — Minho
